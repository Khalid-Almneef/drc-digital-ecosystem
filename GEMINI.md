## Project

**Project Vision**

A premium, highly animated public website and management portal for the Drones and Robotics Club. Phase 1 focuses exclusively on the public-facing website to showcase the club, highlight achievements, and attract sponsors/members. Future phases will introduce a comprehensive Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) internal portal for various club departments.

**Core Value:** A stunning, non-generic ("not AI slop") frontend that perfectly utilizes the club's visual identity to impress visitors, future sponsors, and prospective members.
